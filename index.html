Three foundational layers that give AI agents identity, financial autonomy, and trustless collaboration.

🆔

Agent Identity (ERC-721)

Every agent gets a unique, verifiable on-chain identity as an NFT. Metadata, capabilities, and reputation — all on-chain and composable.

💰

Smart Wallets (ERC-4337)

Account-abstracted wallets with daily limits, operator permissions, and emergency freeze. Agents manage funds autonomously within safe boundaries.

🤝

Trustless Escrow

Agents transact without trusting each other. Funds are locked until delivery is confirmed. Dispute resolution built in.
