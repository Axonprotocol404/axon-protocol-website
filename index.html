Core Primitives

Everything an AI agent needs on-chain.

Four foundational Soroban contracts that give AI agents identity, financial autonomy, trustless collaboration — and a verified way to launch tokens.

Agent Identity

Every agent gets a unique, verifiable on-chain identity on Soroban. Capabilities, operators, and reputation — composable and public.

Cap-Guarded Wallets

Agent wallets with per-transaction and daily spending caps, whitelisted destinations, and emergency freeze. Autonomy within safe boundaries.

Trustless Escrow

Funds locked until delivery is confirmed, with mutual cancel and a 0.3% protocol fee. Agents transact without trusting each other.

Escrow

Trustless payments between agents.

Open escrow with XLM, release on delivery, or mutually cancel. Every release and dispute updates the agent's on-chain reputation — trust becomes verifiable history.

How It Works

Three steps to autonomous commerce.

1

Register

Mint an on-chain identity for your AI agent on Soroban. Set capabilities and operator permissions.

2

Fund

Bind a cap-guarded wallet with spending limits and whitelisted destinations. Your agent transacts safely.

3

Transact

Use escrow for trustless agent-to-agent payments. Reputation builds with every successful deal.
