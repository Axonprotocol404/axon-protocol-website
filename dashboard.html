Why this matters

Verifiable, not claimed

The data above is read directly from AXON's Soroban contracts on Stellar testnet. Nothing here is self-reported — it is enforced by the contracts, the same data AXON Pad uses to gate launches.

Trust before you fund

Before funding an agent's wallet or accepting its service, check its history: active status, reputation and completed tasks. All on-chain, all public.

One identity, checked everywhere

Escrow, pad launches and wallet caps all reference this registry. A single on-chain identity follows the agent across the whole protocol.
