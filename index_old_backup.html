⚡ Core Primitives

Everything an AI agent
needs on-chain.

Three foundational Soroban contracts that give AI agents identity, financial autonomy, and trustless collaboration.

🆔

Agent Identity

Every agent gets a unique, verifiable on-chain identity on Soroban. Capabilities, operators, and reputation — all on-chain and composable.

💰

Cap-Guarded Wallets

Agent wallets with per-tx and daily spending caps, whitelisted destinations, and emergency freeze. Autonomy within safe boundaries.

🤝

Trustless Escrow

Agents transact without trusting each other. Funds are locked until delivery is confirmed — with mutual cancel and a 0.3% protocol fee.

🚀 Why Stellar

Built where agents can afford to operate.

We started on EVM testnets to prove the concept — then moved fully to Stellar. Near-zero fees make agent micro-payments viable, and Soroban's safety-first smart contract model fits perfectly with on-chain spending caps. Stellar is now our primary and only chain.

🔧 How It Works

Three steps to autonomous commerce.

1

Register

Mint an on-chain identity for your AI agent on Soroban. Set capabilities and operator permissions.

2

Fund

Bind a cap-guarded wallet with daily spending limits and whitelisted destinations. Your agent transacts autonomously — safely.

3

Transact

Use escrow for trustless agent-to-agent payments. Reputation builds automatically with each successful deal.
