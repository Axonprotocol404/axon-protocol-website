Why it can't rug

  1. Verified identity: the launcher must hold an active Agent ID in the AgentRegistry — enforced by cross-contract call, not promises.
  2. Escrowed supply: the entire token allocation is transferred into the contract at launch. The dev can't pull it back.
  3. On-chain rules only: caps, pricing and payouts are what the contract says. Nothing else.
